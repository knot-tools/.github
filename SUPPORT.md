# Support

*🇬🇧 English · [🇫🇷 Lire en français](SUPPORT.fr.md)*

> **Knot is in private beta.** Source code lives in private repositories during the beta. The fastest way to reach us is by email.

## Where to ask what

| You want to… | Best place |
|---|---|
| 📰 &nbsp; **Hear about the next public release** | Subscribe via the form on **[knot.tools](https://knot.tools)** |
| 🧪 &nbsp; **Join the private beta** | Email **`contact@knot.tools`** with a brief description of your Dolibarr setup |
| 💡 &nbsp; **Suggest a feature, ask a question, share a use case** | **`contact@knot.tools`** — a real human reads it |
| 🐞 &nbsp; **Report a bug** *(non-security)* | **`contact@knot.tools`** with: Knot version, Dolibarr version, PHP version, reproduction steps, expurgated logs |
| 🔐 &nbsp; **Report a security vulnerability** | **`security@knot.tools`** — see the [security policy](SECURITY.md) and [`security.txt`](https://knot.tools/.well-known/security.txt). **Do not open a public issue.** |
| 📚 &nbsp; **Read the documentation** | Documentation lives inside the private repositories during the beta. Beta testers receive a packaged copy. Selected guides are mirrored on **[knot.tools](https://knot.tools)** as the public catalogue grows. |
| 🤝 &nbsp; **Contribute code** | See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). External pull requests are deferred until **Knot Core** is released publicly. |
| 🏷️ &nbsp; **Use the Knot Tools brand or logo** | Email **`contact@knot.tools`** with a description of the intended usage. The brand pack is documented in the **Knot Core** repository (`img/brand/`). |

## Response times

- **Acknowledgement of any email**: within 72 business hours, by a human.
- **Beta-tester onboarding**: within 5 business days.
- **Security reports**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — same 72-hour acknowledgement, faster path for confirmed criticals.

## What we do not provide

- **Free integration consulting** at scale. We can answer focused questions but cannot turn into an unpaid SaaS support desk.
- **Telemetry**. We do not collect usage data from your installation. If you want us to look at a problem, please send the relevant artefacts (logs with secrets redacted) yourself.
- **Real-time chat or call** during the beta. Email is the channel.
